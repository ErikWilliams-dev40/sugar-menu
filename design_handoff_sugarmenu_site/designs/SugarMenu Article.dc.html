The Solo Trip That Changed Everything About Me

I booked a table for one in Positano, ordered the whole menu, and finally met the woman I’d been too busy to get to know.

By Delphine ReyesOct 20, 202611 min read
ShareIGXLink

The first night, I almost ordered room service. The second night, I put on the green dress I’d packed “just in case,” walked down 211 steps and asked for the best table in the house. The waiter looked behind me for my husband. I let him look.

Nobody tells you that solo travel at this age isn’t about finding yourself. You already know where you are. It’s about finally having the time to listen to her.

“The waiter looked behind me for my husband. I let him look.”

The rules I broke (all of them)

Dessert first. Two glasses of wine at lunch. A nap at four. A conversation with a stranger that lasted until the restaurant stacked the chairs around us.

By the end of the week I’d stopped checking my phone at dinner. By the end of the month I’d booked the next trip. Lisbon, this time. Table for one. Obviously.
